# PRD — curated insertions: decision fatigue as the underlying condition

*Curated to fit the existing flow and voice of "PRD - Marg AI.docx" — not a paste-in dump.
Three small, sourced insertions that strengthen the argument the PRD already makes, without
diluting its Class-X / Bangalore-CBSE focus. Keeps the PRD's discipline of flagging when a figure
is not about the MVP cohort.*

---

### 1 · Insert into §1 (The Problem Space) — after "THE TENSION"

One sentence that names the condition beneath the specific bottleneck and links it to the
persona-engine vision (the "all life stages" roadmap already noted as out-of-scope):

> **The condition underneath.** The after-Class-X freeze is one acute instance of a measured,
> population-wide problem — *decision fatigue*: 1 in 3 Indian adults report decision paralysis and
> 69% struggle to plan ahead amid rapid change (Financial Express, 2024). Marg's wedge is the
> student transition; the underlying job — reducing cognitive load at life's crossroads — is what
> makes the one engine extensible to every later stage.

---

### 2 · Insert into §4 (Secondary Research) — a short block after "THE GUIDANCE GAP"

Kept brief and clearly flagged as adult-population, so it corroborates the *condition* without
being mistaken for the student market (the PRD already owns the student figures — 57% confused,
10.4% guided):

> **THE CONDITION AT POPULATION SCALE (context)**
> Beyond the student cohort, decision fatigue is measured across Indian adults: 33% report decision
> paralysis, 62% feel ill-equipped to decide, and 57% regret opportunities missed by delaying
> (Financial Express survey, 2024).
> *So what:* the guidance gap (above) is why students are stuck; decision fatigue is *why it hurts*
> — and why the problem recurs at every later stage, validating the persona-engine roadmap.
> ⚠ FLAG: This is an adult-population survey, distinct from the Class-X cohort and its 57%-confused
> figure (MetaApply) — do not merge the two. Cited as context for the condition, not MVP sizing.

---

### 3 · Insert into §14 (Policy Alignment & Guardrails) — a note after the NEP table

Strengthens the "policy fit is a hard requirement" case: the state itself treats decision fatigue
as real, which legitimises a decision-support product:

> **THE STATE RECOGNISES DECISION FATIGUE.** Beyond NEP's push for choice, government bodies now
> act on the cognitive cost of choice directly: DARPG is delayering decision levels and moving files
> to e-Office to cut decision bottlenecks; the Ministry of Communications (via NICF / NCA-Finance)
> runs *"Strengthening Cognitive Wellbeing for Effective Decision-Making"* training for public
> servants. *So what:* a citizen-facing tool that lowers decision load is aligned with an active
> government priority — Marg is its complement for personal life choices, not a novelty.
