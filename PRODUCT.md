# Marg AI — Product truth

**Tagline:** Guiding India's Life Choices.

**What it is.** A calm, AI-assisted "life choices roadmap" that helps people see *all*
their real options at a life crossroads and choose for themselves — without being boxed in.
One extensible engine serving every life stage as a config-driven "persona pack": Class 10
student (built, the reference persona) plus prototype personas for Mid-career and Retiree.

**The problem (from the proposal).** People across life stages make major decisions without
structured guidance — leading to misaligned choices, **underused government schemes**, stress,
and weak intergenerational knowledge transfer. Government touches every life stage but offers
*transactions*, not *decision support*. Marg turns the state from service-provider into a
**decision enabler**.

**Who it's for.** Indian citizens at a crossroads — primarily a Class-10 student (and their
parent) choosing what's next after CBSE Class 10 in Bengaluru; extensible to young adults
rethinking careers and seniors shaping later life. Secondary audience: public-sector /
institutional stakeholders who care that it advances national goals.

**The single job of the homepage (this surface).** Be the warm, trustworthy front door anyone
with the app link reaches: say what Marg is in a crisp, warm breath; offer a clear way in
(log in / get started); and signal that it helps citizens actually *use* India's public
initiatives.

**Core principles (non-negotiable, they define the brand).**
- Never a verdict — it shows the whole field and honours the person's own choice.
- Anti-labeling, growth-framed — interests and circumstances can change.
- Expansion as a feature — always surfaces at least one option they hadn't considered, gently.
- Honesty with routes — names real cost/effort, and the real ways through it.
- Non-judgment & privacy — DPDP consent, private data, delete anytime.
- Augments, never replaces, real human counsel.

**Aligns with / helps citizens navigate (from the proposal — real initiatives).**
- **National Education Policy (NEP 2020)** — life-skills & holistic development.
- **Skill India Mission** — career alignment & employability.
- **Digital India** — citizen-centric digital services.
- Scheme & portal navigation: scholarships (National Scholarship Portal), state education
  (DTE / PUE Karnataka), senior support (Elderline). Ministries of Education, Social Justice
  & Empowerment, and Electronics & IT.

**Brand assets.** The Marg logo — a sunrise over a winding road (blue & green paths), wordmark
"Marg", tagline "Guiding India's Life Choices". Warmth, journey, dawn, forward motion.

**Status.** MVP prototype; all option data is provisional/unverified under a blanket
"double-check before you rely on it" disclaimer. Honest about being early.

**Incumbent visual world (inherit & extend for this landing).** React + Vite app. Tokens in
`web/src/index.css`: primary teal `#0E7C7B`, violet `#6D5AC0`, amber `#B4692B`, warm ground
`#F7F5F0`, ink `#233039`. Display face **Spectral** (serif), body **Public Sans**. Calm,
generous, warm, minimal.
